Research and side projects

Research · eLife, 2017

Readability of scientific texts

In a dataset of over 700,000 scientific abstracts published between 1881 and 2015, we found that the readability of scientific texts is steadily declining. Our article was published open access in eLife and covered internationally, for example by Nature News, The Wire and Discover Magazine.

Side project · Recommender system

Letterboxd movie & user recommender

A movie and user recommender system for members of the film social network Letterboxd. Anyone who had rated enough films (about 100+) could search for their username and get recommendations for movies and like-minded users based on their own ratings — or see recommendations for a random user.

Side project · NLP & visualisation

Semantic similarity of scientific journals

Based on abstracts from 123 scientific journals, I used the cortical.io API to construct a semantic fingerprint for each journal and built an interactive comparison matrix in Python with Bokeh.

PhD research

Modelling latent decision processes

In my PhD I used computational models such as reinforcement learning and drift diffusion models, fitted to reaction times and accuracy, together with neuroimaging analyses to make inferences about the decision processes underlying adaptation to errors.

  • Drift diffusion modelling
  • Reinforcement learning modelling

Postdoc · Karolinska Institutet

Standardized neuroimaging preprocessing

As a postdoc at KI I built an fMRI preprocessing and quality-control pipeline on Docker/Singularity containers and BIDS tools, for large-scale analysis of dynamic functional connectivity in chronic pain.
